# pass-0: THETA S on the rover, 22 Nov 2026

1. **Input.** 49 stitched equirectangular panoramas, 5376x2688, one per 8 s; one straight line 6.57 lens heights long (0.8% off straight), steps of 0.08 then 0.17 lens heights; neighbours share 806 SIFT matches and matched directions move 0.68 deg per step; the rover hides 26.3% of the sphere; auto exposure drifts 1.0 stop.
2. **Worked: poses.** 8 pinhole faces per panorama (90 deg, 1600 px) as one rigid COLMAP rig: 49/49 panoramas, 392/392 faces, 147,036 points, 1.38 px = 0.075 deg mean reprojection error (phone run: 275/275 frames, 1.49 px = 0.029 deg). OpenMVG's native spherical model: 49/49, 0.064 deg, and it agrees with the rig to 0.08% of the path length and 0.06 deg. No seam mask needed: 0.077 deg within 5 deg of the seam vs 0.070 far from it, though only 29% of keypoints triangulate there vs 55%.
3. **Worked partly: walls and mesh.** PatchMatch on 17 panoramas gives a depth on 54.6% of pixels and 33.4% of blank bright ones (phone: 55.6% and 8.1%). A monocular prior tied to PatchMatch (2.8% off on held-out blocks, 12.5% with one scale per face) plus floor, ceiling and 8 wall planes fill the rest: 85.0% of blank pixels confirmed from a second panorama (plane pixels 96%, prior-only pixels 74%). Mesh 1.36M -> 2.59M triangles, covering 62.5% -> 90.6% of 196 real views.
4. **Failed.** Splat: 35.0 dB / SSIM 0.935 on held-out panoramas inside the path, 27.2 dB on the 5 beyond its end (at most 0.9 lens heights away), smeared from 3x the lens height up; the depth prior made it worse (34.7 / 26.2 dB). The metric depth network puts the lens at 49 cm (34-66), which would make the ceiling 7.9 m. Scale error not measured: no camera height was given (internal check: the 49 lens heights above the fitted floor spread by 0.84%).
5. **Next.** Tape three numbers for the scale error: lens height, ceiling (model: 16.11 lens heights), radiator wall to wood panel (5.96). Reshoot as a closed loop through the kitchen itself, one shot per 15-20 cm instead of 0.17 lens heights, a second pass from a 1.0-1.3 m mast, exposure locked; test the splat on that second pass, not on held-out neighbours.
