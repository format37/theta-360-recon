# pool: THETA S on a hand-held pole, a walk round the pool building, 23 Nov 2026

1. **Input.** 144 stitched equirectangular panoramas, 5376x2688, one per 8 s over 19 min; a walk of 768 lens heights, twice round the building and through its car parks, 5.2 lens heights from shot to shot (walking, not stop and go); the operator and a companion in every shot (masked by a segmentation network, with the sky); auto exposure, 2.3 stops; the camera levels its stills with an accelerometer that walking shakes (4.6 deg median, 24 at most). No GPS worth using (3 positions in 144 photos), no tape number.
2. **Worked: poses.** 144/144 panoramas in one rig model, 127,842 points, 0.074 deg mean error (pass-0: 0.075); 25,175 face pairs chosen on the GPU instead of 660,000, 699 loop closures, mapper 16 min. OpenMVG's spherical model agrees to 0.22% of the walk and 0.34 deg, except R0013079 (under the pines, 97 points), which the rig mapper had put 17 lens heights off the walk: taken out, 143 panoramas from there on.
3. **Worked partly: mesh and voxels.** PatchMatch on every panorama (589 faces at 768 px, 1 h 52 min) keeps a depth on 13% of the pixels only (pass-0: 55%): from shots 5-10 lens heights apart a surface looks too different. The outdoor depth prior fills 98%, but a second panorama confirms only 18% of what it fills, and far buildings come back as phantom sheets. Mesh: 16.1M triangles, covers 64% of the real views; facades, window bands and the roof edge read. Voxels: 206k of 0.1 lens heights, free space carved, 2+ panoramas each; the facades stand, the ground is patchy.
4. **Worked best: splat.** 2.5M Gaussians started from the fused cloud, a learned sky, trained in a normalised frame: 17.2 dB / SSIM 0.61 on held-out panoramas between shots, 14.5 dB beyond the end of the walk (19.1 / 17.9 with the sky); the whole site reads from above, near the camera it smears; depth supervision adds 0.7 dB beyond the walk. Trained on the first two loops and tested on the third pass (32 panoramas): 16.2 dB / SSIM 0.53 / LPIPS 0.49; 17.4 dB within 2 lens heights of a trained shot, 14.2 at 10-17. The roof was never in view: it is noise in every product. The building is 71.0 x 24.8 lens heights between facade lines.
5. **Next.** Tape one number for metres: the lens height above the ground, or the building's length. Reshoot stop and go, 2 lens heights (3-4 m) between shots, a second loop farther out, exposure locked; the roof from a raised view (drone or a 4-5 m pole).
